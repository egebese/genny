# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the person you are replying to is trying to help.

## What we expect

- Critique code, not people. "This query bypasses RLS" is useful. "This is
  garbage" is not.
- Assume good faith on both sides of a review.
- Accept that maintainers say no to things that are fine but out of scope.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, sexualised language, publishing someone's private
information, and sustained disruption of discussion.

## Enforcement

Report to ege@fal.ai. Reports stay private. Maintainers will respond, and may
edit, remove, or ban as needed. Maintainers who break these rules are held to
them too.

Adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant, shortened deliberately: a policy
nobody reads protects nobody.
